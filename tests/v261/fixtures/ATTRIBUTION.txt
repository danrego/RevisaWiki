The wikitext fixtures in this directory were captured from Portuguese
Wikipedia at the permanent revisions listed in source_revisions.json.
Their source URLs, dates, revision identifiers and original SHA-256 values
are recorded there. They are reference material under Wikipedia's applicable
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license. Attribution and revision
history can be consulted through each permanent source URL and its history.

The synthetic examples in run_focus.cjs contain no actual account identity
or personal information. Scientific, article and bibliographic source data
in the public-page fixtures remain source material.
